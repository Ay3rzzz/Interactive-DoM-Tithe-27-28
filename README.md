# McMaster Department of Medicine Interactive Tithe Model

Use the calculator to see how your estimated annual payment changes when you enter the same billings under McMaster’s current tithe model and the illustrative 2027–28 academic year model. Both totals include your $7,000 annual membership fee.

## Enter Your Annual Billings

Type your annual billings in Canadian dollars, move the slider, or select **Try $365,000** or **Try $700,000** to explore an example. The slider goes up to $2 million. For higher amounts, type directly into the billing field. Your billing entry stays in your browser and is never saved by the calculator.

## Read Your Results

At **$365,000** in annual billings, your estimated payment is **$28,750** under the current model and **$31,107** under the 2027–28 model. That’s **$2,357 more per year** at the same billing level.

Each coloured bar shows how the total adds up. The gold segment is your membership fee. Select **View Brackets and Calculations** to see the rate and dollar amount for each bracket. Each rate applies only to the portion of your billings within that bracket.

Select **Compare Ottawa and London** to see what those models would calculate using the same amount. The types of income each institution includes can differ.
